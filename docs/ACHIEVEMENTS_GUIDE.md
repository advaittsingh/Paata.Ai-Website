# How to Earn Achievements and Badges

## Overview

PAATA.AI has a comprehensive achievement and badge system that rewards you for using the platform and reaching learning milestones. Achievements and badges are automatically awarded when you meet the criteria.

## How It Works

### Automatic Awarding

Achievements and badges are **automatically checked and awarded** when you:
- Create notes
- Create flashcards
- Create mind maps
- Complete exam sessions
- Complete focus sessions
- Use chat (interactions tracked automatically)

You can also manually trigger achievement checking by calling `/api/achievements/check` endpoint.

## Tips for Earning Achievements

1. **Be Consistent:** Daily usage helps maintain streaks
2. **Use All Features:** Try notes, flashcards, mind maps, exams, and focus mode
3. **Set Goals:** Aim for specific achievements to stay motivated
4. **Track Progress:** Check the Achievements page regularly to see your progress
5. **Practice Regularly:** Complete exams and focus sessions to earn related achievements

## Viewing Your Achievements

1. Go to the **Achievements** page from the Smart Learning sidebar
2. View your progress on each achievement
3. See which badges you've earned
4. Check your total points and unlocked achievements

## Progress Tracking

- Each achievement shows a progress bar (0-100%)
- Progress is calculated automatically based on your activity
- Achievements unlock automatically when you reach 100%
- Badges are awarded when you meet their criteria

## Notes

- Achievements are checked automatically after relevant actions
- Progress is saved in real-time
- You can view all achievements even if not unlocked yet
- Badges are awarded based on achievements and milestones
